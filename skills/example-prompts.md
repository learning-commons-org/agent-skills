# Example Prompts

## Lesson Planning

### Math

- Create a 2nd grade lesson plan that helps students understand how to write addition and subtraction equations that match a given, real world problem. The goal of this lesson is to write equations that match a number story, not necessarily to solve the equation correctly.
- I need a lesson on solving one-step equations for my 6th grade class.
- Plan a lesson on transformation of functions for my Alg 2 class.
- We use Illustrative Mathematics. I'm on the grade 3 unit introducing multiplication — equal groups. Can you build me a supplemental lesson for 3.OA.A.1? My class needs another day on it before we move on.
- Make a lesson on proportional relationships for 7th grade. We're using Eureka Math Module 1.
- 3rd grade fractions on a number line. Tomorrow. My students think 1/4 is bigger than 1/3 and they have never seen a fraction past 1. 45 minutes.
- I'm teaching counting money to my 2nd graders next week — dollar bills, quarters, dimes, nickels, pennies, word problems with $ and cent symbols. Lesson plan plus something they can work on independently.

### ELA

- Create a 2nd grade lesson to help students write a short opinion piece about their favorite book, with a clear reason and a closing sentence.
- I need a lesson for my 7th graders on analyzing how an author develops a central idea across two informational texts. We're in the middle of a unit on environmental issues.
- Plan a lesson for 11th grade where students practice writing a thesis and evidence-based argument paragraph in response to a complex nonfiction text. We're building toward a research essay.
- I'm looking for a first-grade lesson on identifying r-controlled vowels in a spoken one-syllable words. I teach in New York.

### Science

- Create a 3rd grade NGSS-aligned lesson on the life cycle of plants — I want students to sequence the stages and explain what each plant needs to survive.
- I need a lesson for 6th grade on how energy is transferred in food webs. We're at the start of our ecosystems unit.
- Create a lesson plan on periodic trends, especially how electronegativity impacts bonding, for Texas high school chemistry.

### Social studies

- I need a lesson for 4th grade on how geography influenced where early communities settled in California. We're about halfway through our state history unit.
- Create a lesson for 8th grade on the causes of the American Revolution — I want students to evaluate which cause was most significant and back it up with evidence. We're in Texas.
- Plan a lesson for 11th grade US History on the economic and social impacts of the Great Migration. We're in our early 20th century unit and I want students working with primary sources. I teach in Oklahoma.
